📚 Sources used in this comparison

▾

Every cost, duration, and program-structure fact below is drawn from the sources listed here — mostly official provider pages and independent review sites checked in July 2026. Where no hard citable figure exists (marked ESTIMATE in the table), we say so explicitly rather than inventing a number.

    🔍 How this was researched

    ▾

    Cost — Taken directly from each provider's official pricing page or FAQ (Coursera's Coursera Support page, MIT's MicroMasters FAQ via Zendesk, Udacity's own pricing page, freeCodeCamp's public/free curriculum). Where a provider offers multiple payment paths (monthly subscription vs. bundle vs. pay-per-course), we used the most commonly cited total cost to complete the full credential.

    Time to complete — Sourced from official program pages stating expected weekly hours and total duration. freeCodeCamp's ~300-hour estimate comes from a completed-certificate record and a third-party course summary rather than an official freeCodeCamp duration page, so it's treated as a reasonable but secondary-sourced figure.

    Employer recognition and Job placement support — These can't be reduced to a single citable number, so they are analyst judgments informed by sourced facts (e.g. Google's employer consortium and 2.4M+ enrollee base; MIT's small credential-holder pool of ~1,586 as of 2024; Udacity's industry-partner curriculum and Accenture acquisition; freeCodeCamp's lack of a formal placement program). These are flagged as ESTIMATE throughout.

    Content depth — Assessed from each provider's published curriculum description (tools taught, prerequisite level, capstone requirements). Flagged as ESTIMATE since "depth" is inherently qualitative.

    Conflicts we resolved: Sources gave a range of Google Data Analytics Certificate costs ($147 fast-track to $399/yr Coursera Plus). We used the officially quoted ~6-month, $294 pace as the baseline since it matches Coursera's own stated median timeline. Similarly, Udacity's Data Analyst Nanodegree price has shifted across pricing-model changes ($399/mo individual → $249/mo all-access with a $846 four-month bundle); we used the current 2026 all-access bundle price as the most representative figure. MIT's MicroMasters has no single official "expected months" figure in the sources we found, so total time is marked as an estimate based on typical graduate-course pacing rather than an official MIT number.
    Built for informational comparison purposes only — always verify current pricing and program details directly with each provider before enrolling. Not financial, legal, or career advice.
