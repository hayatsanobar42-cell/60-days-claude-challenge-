Money tips for students on a fixed allowance
💡
Pay yourself first — move your savings percentage the day allowance lands, before you spend anything.
💡
Use the envelope method: split your allowance into fixed pots for food, transport, and fun so no single category eats the rest.
💡
Track subscriptions monthly — ₹119 feels small, but three unused subscriptions add up to a real dent over a year.
💡
Set one visible goal at a time. Chasing five goals at once usually means funding none of them properly.
💡
Review your spending log weekly, not just at month end — small course corrections are easier than a big one.
