# Day 30 – Supply Chain Optimizer

## Objective
Built an interactive Supply Chain Optimizer that simulates real-world supply chain decision-making.

## Features
- Supplier Strategy Selection
- Factory Location Planning
- Warehouse Optimization
- Transportation Strategy
- Inventory Management
- Business Metrics Dashboard
- Supply Chain Performance Score

## What I Learned
- Supplier diversification reduces risk.
- Factory location affects production cost.
- Warehouse strategy impacts delivery speed.
- Transportation influences logistics expenses.
- Inventory management balances cost and availability.
- Small operational decisions can significantly affect business performance.

## Outcome
Successfully completed the Supply Chain Optimization simulator and analyzed how strategic decisions influence operational efficiency.
