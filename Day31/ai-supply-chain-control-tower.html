Mission Briefing

You are the Head of Operations for a global supply chain. For 3 minutes, alerts will stream in from across your network — ports, factories, warehouses, and carriers. Your job is to triage and resolve them before impact escalates.

How it works

  • Each alert has a priority, a countdown, and a business impact.
  • Pick an action button to resolve it. The best action improves KPIs and score; weak or wrong actions hurt them.
  • If an alert's timer runs out unresolved, it auto-escalates and damages your KPIs.
  • Some choices have delayed consequences that show up seconds later in the Event Log.
  • As time passes, alerts arrive faster and more can be active at once.

KPIs to protect

  • Service Level, Customer Satisfaction, Inventory Health, Transportation Efficiency — keep these high.
  • Operating Cost — keep this low.
  • Revenue Protected and Score — keep these climbing.

Scoring

Your final grade (A+ to D) is based on your score and average KPI health at the end of the shift.
