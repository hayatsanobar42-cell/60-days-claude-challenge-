# Day 22 – Validate Your Startup Idea Like a VC

## Startup Idea
StudySync AI – AI-powered study planner for students.

## Objective
Validate a startup idea using a VC-style framework.

## Key Learnings

- Customer problems should be validated before building.
- Market size helps estimate growth potential.
- Competitor analysis reveals market gaps.
- User feedback is essential for product-market fit.

## Outcome

Claude generated a complete startup validation report covering:

- Founder-Market Fit
- TAM, SAM, SOM
- Competitor Analysis
- Customer Persona
- Risk Assessment
- Go / No-Go Recommendation
- 30-Day Validation Plan
