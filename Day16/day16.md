Day 16 – Build Your First Stock Research Skill

Objective

Created a reusable Claude Custom Skill for stock fundamental analysis.

Skill Details

- Skill Name: stock-fundamental-research
- Focus: Indian and global listed companies
- Analysis Modes:
  - Quick Take
  - Deep Dive
  - Compare
  - Pros & Cons
  - Portfolio Fit

Key Features

- Live data prioritization
- Multi-source verification
- Fundamental ratio analysis
- Financial health assessment
- Peer comparison
- Ownership analysis
- Risk identification
- Price chart generation

Tested Companies

- TCS (Quick Take)
- Reliance Industries (Deep Dive)

«Note: Compare mode could not be tested due to Claude usage limits.»

Key Learnings

- Learned how to create reusable Claude Skills.
- Understood the importance of evidence-based stock research.
- Explored fundamental metrics such as P/E, ROE, ROCE, and debt ratios.
- Practiced building structured AI workflows instead of repeating prompts.
