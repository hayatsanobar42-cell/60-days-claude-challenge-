# Day 3 - Role Based Prompting

## Question
How can I learn Prompt Engineering in 30 days?

## Normal Response
The response provided a general 30-day roadmap for learning Prompt Engineering. It covered fundamentals, prompt patterns, practical techniques, real-world applications, and advanced concepts. The focus was on building a strong foundation through daily practice and experimentation.

## Founder Persona Response
The response approached Prompt Engineering from a startup founder's perspective. It emphasized solving business problems, creating valuable products, improving productivity, understanding customer needs, and using AI to support business growth and decision-making.

## Developer Persona Response
The response approached Prompt Engineering from a software developer's perspective. It focused on APIs, prompt design techniques, testing, debugging, automation, workflows, and building hands-on projects. The goal was to develop technical expertise and practical implementation skills.

## Comparison

- Normal: General advice
- Founder: Business and startup focused advice
- Developer: Technical and implementation focused advice

## Learning
Role-based prompting significantly changes the quality and focus of AI responses. When no role was assigned, the response was general and suitable for everyone. The Founder persona provided a business-oriented perspective, while the Developer persona provided a technical and implementation-focused perspective. This demonstrates that assigning a role helps AI generate more targeted, relevant, and useful responses for specific goals.
