Final Verdict

⚠️ Apply With Caution

This internship appears suitable for gaining practical Data Analytics experience. However, the company's credibility and long-term opportunities should be verified before accepting an offer.

Smart Interview Questions

1. Can you share details about the company's history, team size, and major clients?
2. Are there opportunities for full-time conversion after the internship?
3. What are the criteria for receiving a performance-based recommendation letter?
4. What are the expected working hours and timezone requirements?
5. How is mentorship structured for interns?

How to Proceed

Before Applying

- Research the company online
- Check LinkedIn, Glassdoor, and company website
- Verify company legitimacy and employee reviews

During Interview

- Ask clarification questions regarding mentorship and expectations
- Confirm work schedule and reporting structure
- Understand internship evaluation criteria

If Offered

- Obtain stipend and internship details in writing
- Clarify recommendation letter requirements
- Confirm duration and responsibilities
